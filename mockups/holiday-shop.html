Mockup: holiday shop (WO 0001)

Static mockup, not the app. Black and orange stays the brand; Christmas is accents only (deep red and pine green candy-stripe ribbon, a corner ribbon on the stands, gift copy, a light CSS snowfall that turns off with reduced-motion). No delivery dates or "arrives by" promises anywhere. Monthly-payment messaging for Affirm and Klarna comes from Stripe's own element, shown here as a dashed placeholder, never hand-written numbers. Each block is shown at desktop (1180px) and phone (390px).
